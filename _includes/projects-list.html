Can Follow-Up Questions Help Weak-to-Strong Monitors for Swarms?

Apart Research · Swarm Alignment Hackathon · Nov 2026

A weak, trusted monitor loses track of false progress claims as a swarm grows (AUROC 0.75 → 0.65 from 1 to 50 agents). Letting it ask one follow-up question and check the answer against the logs lifts it to 0.93 / 0.88, recovering 76–82% of the gap to a strong monitor.

Fragile or Just Hard? Robustness Prediction from Reasoning Probes

AIMO Interpretability Challenge · 2026

Can we tell from a model's hidden states whether its answer will survive a small rewording? A linear probe on one greedy reasoning trace matches eight sampled 16k-token solutions (0.774 vs 0.772 accuracy), and the full stack reaches 0.831. Robustness turns out to be related to competence, but not the same thing.

A shortcut-prone model organism sometimes exits early on misleading cues, so the steps that would expose a wrong answer never appear. I'm building a small investigator that asks targeted follow-up questions, and testing it against passive judges and debate under fixed budgets.
